About RainGuard

More Than Just
Rain Gear.

We're a team of outdoor obsessives, city commuters, and design nerds who believe rain shouldn't stop you from doing what you love. Since 2018, we've been engineering gear that actually works when the sky opens up.

Our Story Get in Touch
Founded in Portland, OR (2018) • Field-Tested Worldwide
Our Mission

To make storm-proof, sustainably made rain gear accessible to everyone — so no one has to choose between staying dry and stepping outside.

Built with care, tested in storms
📖 The Journey

How RainGuard Began

From a leaky garage workshop to a trusted rain gear brand.

2018

The Garage Prototype

Frustrated by umbrellas that flipped inside out, our founder built the first StormGuard prototype in a Portland garage.

2019

First 1,000 Customers

Word spread fast. We sold our first 1,000 umbrellas to commuters and hikers across the Pacific Northwest.

2021

Expanded to Apparel

We launched our first line of raincoats and boots, bringing the same storm-tested philosophy to full-body protection.

Today

25,000+ Dry Customers

We now serve customers in 12 countries, with a full range of umbrellas, jackets, boots, and corporate branding services.

💡 What Drives Us

Our Core Values

Every product decision, every partnership, and every stitch is guided by these principles.

Uncompromising Quality

Every product is storm-tested in real conditions before it earns the RainGuard name. No shortcuts.

Sustainable Materials

We use PFAS-free coatings and recycled fabrics wherever possible. Better gear, smaller footprint.

Customer First

Free returns, 2-year warranty, and a real human on the other end of every email. We stand by our gear.

Constant Innovation

We iterate relentlessly. Each season's gear is a little lighter, a little tougher, a little smarter.
